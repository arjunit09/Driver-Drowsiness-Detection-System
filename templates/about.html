Automotive Safety System

About Smart Driver Monitor

An intelligent, real-time computer vision safety system designed to prevent road accidents caused by driver fatigue, microsleeps, and yawning.

How The System Works

1. Frame Capture

Captures live webcam feed at 640x480 resolution using OpenCV and DirectShow hardware integration.

2. Landmark Mesh

Uses dlib shape predictor to detect 68 2D facial landmark coordinates in real time.

3. Metric Engine

Calculates Eye Aspect Ratio ($\text{EAR} < 0.25$) and Mouth Aspect Ratio ($\text{MAR} > 0.15$) continuously.

4. Dual Alarm & Audit

Triggers multi-channel audio alarms and logs incident timestamps to SQLite & analytics dashboard.
